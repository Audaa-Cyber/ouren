# Ouren brand assets

## Replace the logo

Upload your own transparent PNG image to this folder with this exact filename:

`ouren-logo.png`

Final path:

`assets/ouren-logo.png`

The app is already configured to look for that URL. You do not need to edit `App.js`; once the image is committed to the `main` branch, reload the app. Use a transparent PNG with the logo artwork in a single color (black or white); the app tints it to suit light and dark screens.

Recommended image: square canvas, 512 × 512 px or larger, with a little transparent padding around the mark. Keep the mark centered.

Until you upload the image, the app falls back to its temporary drawn mark.
